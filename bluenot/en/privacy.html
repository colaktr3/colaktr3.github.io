In short

BlueNot does not collect any personal data, does not send it to any server and does not share it with third parties. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Data stored

Your income, expense, subscription and group records are stored in the app's folder on your device. Summary amounts for the home screen widget are written to a shared app area on the same device. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app. If device backup (iCloud or computer) is on, it may be included in Apple's backups.

Face ID

The Face ID lock uses Apple's LocalAuthentication. Your face data never reaches BlueNot; verification is done entirely by iOS.

Notifications

Payment reminders are local notifications created on your device; no server is used.

Subscriptions and payments

Purchases and subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple (App Store). We never see or store your payment details. You can manage or cancel your subscription in iPhone Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.

Analytics and ads

The app contains no analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from children; it collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the update will be published on this page.
