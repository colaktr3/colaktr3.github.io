FAQ

Where is my data stored?
Only on your iPhone. It is never sent to a server.

How do I add a recurring payment?
In the Entries tab, tap + to add an item, make it recurring and enter how many months it lasts. It's added to those months automatically.

How do I mark something as paid?
Tap the item and turn on Paid, or swipe right. The amount comes out of "Money left".

What happens when I close a month?
That month's records are locked and can't be edited. You can reopen it anytime.

Free trial and subscription
The first week is free, then the yearly subscription renews through the App Store. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends and you won't be charged.

How do I cancel?
iPhone Settings → Apple Account (your name) → Subscriptions → BlueNot → Cancel Subscription.

I got a new phone. Where is my purchase?
Go to Settings → Subscription → "Restore purchases".
