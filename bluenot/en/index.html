What is BlueNot?

BlueNot keeps your income, bills, loans, installments and subscriptions in one simple monthly ledger. Recurring items are added every month automatically, you mark what you've paid, and "Money left" updates instantly.

  • Recurring items (fixed length or ongoing)
  • Paid marks and due-date reminders
  • Your own groups, drag-and-drop ordering
  • Close a month, monthly balance report
  • Face ID lock and home screen widget
